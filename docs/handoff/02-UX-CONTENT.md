# Website UX and content direction

## Audience and primary objective

Primary decision-maker: parent/guardian (often 30–50). Secondary audience: student in grades 5–10. The site should make parents feel **understood and safe choosing a mentor**, and make children feel welcomed, not judged.

The objective is a meaningful admission enquiry, not a generic traffic metric.

## Five-second hero

Eyebrow: `Vardhaman Academy · Classes 5–10`

Headline: **Every child learns differently. Every child deserves someone who understands.**

Supporting copy: `At Vardhaman Academy, Rushab Sir helps students build understanding, confidence and stronger learning habits.`

CTA: **Tell Us About Your Child**

Support line: `Bansilal Nagar, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar`

Use `assets/hero/rushab-warm-hero-v1.png`; keep the official logo separate in the header. Make CTA destination configurable. Because WhatsApp/admissions is still pending, **do not send or collect enquiry data until an owner-approved workflow is ready**.

## Proposed section order

1. **Hero / first impression:** Warm portrait, reassurance, clear offer and single primary CTA.
2. **Meet Rushab Sir:** Human story, thoughtful teaching style, original speaking photos used honestly as external seminar documentation.
3. **Do you recognise these concerns?** Parent-first questions: child avoids doubts? memorises but forgets? loses confidence? Avoid fear-based language or diagnosing anyone.
4. **How learning happens:** Clear explanations, student questions, practice and feedback. Keep these as a *proposed approach* for Rushab to confirm, not claims about an existing formal programme.
5. **Real student journeys:** Before/after learning-confidence stories only after verified details and consent; prioritise improvement over elite scores.
6. **Beyond textbooks:** Authentic cricket, football and outdoor activity stories. Avoid implying all activities occur every term unless documented.
7. **Parent and student voices:** Actual attributed quotes/video testimonials after permissions; do not invent reviews.
8. **Visit / ask about a batch:** Grade-level information, verified location and a working contact action once configured.

## Interactions: appealing, not distracting

- A gentle scroll reveal, subtle card hover on desktop, and compact photo/video gallery with meaningful filters: `Learning`, `Achievements`, `Activities`, `Seminars`.
- Videos use still posters with click-to-play. No autoplay with sound; do not load every full MP4 on initial paint.
- Support reduced-motion settings, touch and keyboard interaction, visible focus rings, semantic headings and text alternatives.
- Keep mobile navigation minimal; avoid intrusive popups.
- Preview unapproved student media only in a private review environment, not a public site or public preview URL.

## Conversion flow

Future CTA copy: `Tell us your child's class and what's been difficult lately.`

Proposed later form fields (owner approval required): Parent's name, child's class (5–10), subject or concern (optional), preferred contact. Include a privacy notice before data transmission. Until integration is approved, CTA may open an informational contact area, **not a nonfunctional or pretend submission form**.

## Copy and tone

Friendly and reassuring, not artificially emotional. No comparison with other teachers. Avoid `Guaranteed results`, `100% success`, `Your child will become a pilot/doctor`, fake names or unverified ranking.

## Layout quality bar

- Accessible type contrast, no tiny captions, responsive image crops, portrait content not overlaid with dense text.
- A parent can answer in five seconds: **Who teaches? Which grades? Where? Why trust them? What do I do next?**
- Photo captions distinguish the academy's own activities from external seminar appearances.
