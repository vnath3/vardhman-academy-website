## Accuracy and media care

- The seminar photos show the presentation of **Aurangabad Public School**, not an academy-hosted event. If used as documentary images, caption them as Rushab speaking at an external educational seminar; do not imply affiliation or academy ownership of the other school's board results.
- One outdoor-event source showed a `Happy Children's Day` sign; use a generic `Student activities` caption unless the date is verified.
- Supplied JPEGs/MP4s appear WhatsApp-compressed; optimise for the web without over-sharpening faces. Use responsive srcsets and lazy loading.
- For production video hosting, YouTube embeds may be easier later; these selected local MP4s are included for curation, edit previews, and optional self-hosting. Do not upload them to any service without approval.
