## Visual rules

1. Preserve the existing blue-green learner/open-book symbol intact. Source reference: `assets/brand/legacy-brand-poster-img-01.jpeg`. This poster is a visual **reference**, not the production logo.
2. Warm ivory base `#F8F9F3`; medium academic blue approximately `#147BA5`; fresh supportive green approximately `#83C448`; dark ink `#163448`. Values are **provisional design tokens** inspired by the posters, not an official colour specification.
3. Generous whitespace, highly readable body type, 16px+ mobile body text, calm transitions, clear focus states and strong contrasts.
4. Keep the homepage human: one real mentor image, real parent concerns, genuine student stories once approved. Avoid stock-photo-style student imagery or overwhelming animations.
5. Desktop hero: mentor portrait and substantial negative space for copy. Mobile hero: preserve Rushab Sir's face/hand crop, move headline above portrait as needed.
6. Avoid arbitrary 3D effects, loud confetti animations, auto-playing audio, and crowded grids of scores.
