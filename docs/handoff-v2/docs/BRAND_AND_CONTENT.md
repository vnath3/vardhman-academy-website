## Accuracy rules

- Existing **student journey** story cards are clearly labelled **sample/illustrative**. They must not be recast as factual alumni careers until the owner supplies real cases and consent.
- Do not invent result percentages, exam achievements, scholarship rates, parent reviews, institutional affiliations or quotes.
- The photo of Rushab speaking with an Aurangabad Public School projection in the background was taken at an **external educational seminar**. It is truthful proof of a speaking appearance, not a Vardhaman-hosted event. **Do not substitute the projected school logo** with Vardhaman's to change the historical meaning.
- IMG-17 contains a historical Vardhaman banner stating old offers. It may be shown as a dated event record privately but not as a current course list.
- Do not use a school crest/logo seen in flyers as a replacement academy brand.
- Certificate names, percentages and award organizations in historic images must not become typed marketing claims without verification.
- Contact numbers are visible in legacy posters but aren't automatically approved as current public WhatsApp/admissions numbers.

## Deferred until explicitly approved

Real testimonials; external YouTube embeds; WhatsApp deep link; admissions form submission/back-end; Google Analytics/gtag; official domain; public media publishing.
