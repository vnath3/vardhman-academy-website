# V2 design and content decisions

## Confirmed

- **Official name:** Vardhaman Academy (exact spelling; not Vardhman).
- **Current academic range:** Classes **5 through 10**. Ignore legacy 1–12 and XI–XII offers printed on historical posters.
- **Boards/subjects:** all boards and all subjects per project brief; owner to verify specific services before release.
- **Location:** Bansilal Nagar, Chhatrapati Sambhajinagar, Maharashtra.
- **Mentor:** Rushab Mutha / Rushab Sir.
- **Brand:** preserve the real blue-green book-and-three-learners symbol; use the established blue/green mark as the identity anchor. The symbol is not reimagined.
- **Audience:** appeal primarily to parents, while remaining engaging to students.
- **Design direction:** retain the original interactive concept's warm narrative, movement and micro-interactions. Incorporate real academy images and videos where genuine evidence improves trust.
- **Communication tone:** clear, warm, optimistic, confidence-building. Avoid exaggerated claims, invented alumni success stories and unsolicited quotations.

## Current V2 page order

1. Brand and mentor-led introduction with the parent-first call to action.
2. Parent concerns and Rushab Sir's introduction with real seminar photograph.
3. **Life at Vardhaman:** filterable private gallery of sports, achievements, community and video clips.
4. Student journey carousel with clearly marked *illustrative stories*.
5. Learning values, interactive Class 5–10 journey, Brain Spark quiz and 4-step parent enquiry demo.
6. FAQ and footer.

## Presentation guardrails

- A seminar background that shows Aurangabad Public School stays truthful: this photo documents Rushab Sir speaking at an **external educational event**. Do not replace its background with Vardhaman's identity to imply it happened inside the academy.
- Historic academy banners may document a past event but may **not** provide the current course list.
- A student holding a certificate demonstrates an award photograph, but no award name, rank, or academic impact should be stated without verification.
- No testimonials or conversion/revenue claims without verified permission.
- No external YouTube embeds, WhatsApp integration, GA tags, admissions pipeline or official domain deployment has been implemented in V2 yet.
