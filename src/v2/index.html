The heart of Vardhaman

A mentor who truly gets students.

Rushabh Mutha has taught 5,000+ students. He is an engineer by background and a young teacher who stays close to how students think and live today, technology included.

He knows why a bright child switches off in class, why homework gets pushed to “later”, and why that becomes a habit. Instead of pushing harder, he finds the reason and works on it with patience and a plan.

He also believes a child's life is bigger than the classroom. Games, friends, celebrations, birthdays and small gifts are part of the academy, because children learn best when they feel happy, included and noticed.
