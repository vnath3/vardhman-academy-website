Consent and privacy

How we use photos, videos and results.

Last updated 21 November 2026

Our declaration

Photographs, videos and student results on the Vardhaman Academy website are published only with the permission of each student's parent or guardian. Permission was confirmed before anything was added.

How results are shown

Results appear with the student's first name and the first letter of the surname only. We do not publish school names, ranks or photographs next to results.

Photos and videos

Photos and videos of activities, achievements and community moments are shared with the permission of the families of the students shown. We aim to show students in a positive, respectful way.

If you send an enquiry

When you use the enquiry form, we collect your name, your mobile number and your child's class group (1–4, 5–7 or 8–10). We use these only to call or message you about tuition at Vardhaman Academy. They are kept in a private spreadsheet that only the academy can open. We do not sell your details or share them with other businesses.

To have your details deleted, ask Rushabh Sir by call or WhatsApp, using the contact details at the bottom of the website. The website also counts anonymous visits and button taps. That count never includes your name or number.

Please note

  • The photo of Rushabh Sir speaking at a seminar was taken at an external event. The event was not hosted by Vardhaman Academy.
  • An old printed banner in one community photo lists earlier courses. Vardhaman Academy currently teaches Classes 1–10 only.

Want something changed or removed?

If you are a parent or guardian and would like a photo, video or result removed or changed, please message or call us on the number shown in the footer of the main website. We will take it down.

Website analytics

This website uses Google Analytics to count visits and see which sections are used. It does not receive anything you type in the enquiry form. The enquiry message stays on your device until you choose to copy it or open WhatsApp.
